How it began

We Saw What Others Overlooked

It began in an ordinary kitchen in India — not with a recipe, but with a realisation. A small bowl of seeds sat quietly on a shelf, the same seeds that ancient civilisations built their strength upon. Chia. Watermelon. Pumpkin. Packed with Omega-3 fatty acids, zinc, magnesium, and vitamins that most Indians never get enough of — and yet, barely anyone was eating them.

"If a single tablespoon of chia holds more Omega-3 than a glass of milk holds calcium — why isn't every Indian home eating it?"

We couldn't let that question go. So we didn't. We founded our brand not to sell seeds, but to change the way India eats. To make superfoods feel like everyday food. To bring premium quality nutrition to every household — whether in Mumbai, Mysuru, or a small town in Madhya Pradesh.

The mission

A Nutrition Revolution, One Home at a Time

India has one of the highest rates of nutritional deficiency in the world. Not because of poverty alone — but because the right foods never reached the right homes. We set out to change that.


We sourced only the finest seeds. We said no to fillers, no to artificial processing, no to compromise. Every pack that leaves our hands carries a promise: what goes into your family's body is the very best nature has to offer.


Mothers adding chia to their children's morning smoothies. Fathers sprinkling watermelon seeds over their evening salads. Young professionals discovering that the tiny pumpkin seed holds the key to better sleep, stronger immunity, and a calmer mind. This is the India we are building — one seed at a time.
